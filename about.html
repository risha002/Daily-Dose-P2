Our Story

Daily Dose Café is a student-friendly café created to provide a comfortable place where people can relax, enjoy good food, and take a break from their daily routines.


Our café offers freshly brewed coffee, refreshing non-coffee drinks, pastries, and affordable meals. We aim to create a welcoming place for students, employees, coffee lovers, and young adults.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide affordable food and drinks while giving our customers a comfortable and relaxing place to enjoy their time.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a welcoming café where students and the community can relax, connect, and enjoy quality food and drinks.

Our History

Daily Dose Café was developed as a café business concept by a group of Bachelor of Science in Computer Science students. The idea was created around providing affordable food, refreshing drinks, and a comfortable atmosphere for customers.

Meet our Team

Daily Dose Café is managed and developed by a team of students who worked together to create the café concept and website.
